# Responsible AI-assisted delivery

- Status: proposed public messaging approved in principle
- Scope: public website copy and delivery policy
- Principle: modern tools increase the care applied to the work; they do not replace accountability

## Position

The studio uses artificial intelligence, automation, modern development environments, static analysis,
automated testing, performance and accessibility tooling, version control, and repeatable delivery
workflows whenever they improve the result.

This use is not hidden and is not limited to one vendor or conversational assistant. Tools may evolve;
the durable capability is knowing how to select them, frame the problem, provide useful context,
orchestrate complementary tools, challenge their output, detect uncertainty and errors, and verify the
result. That professional judgement creates the faster feedback loop, broader exploration, earlier
defect detection, clearer documentation, and additional time spent understanding the client's need.

AI does not perform the project independently. Using it well is a technical and methodological skill:
poor framing can produce a fast but irrelevant answer, while competent supervision turns provisional
output into tested, maintainable work. Knowing when not to use AI is part of the same expertise.

`Modern` does not mean adopting every novelty. A technology is selected only when it is suitable,
maintainable, secure, and useful for the project. The client pays for an appropriate result and a
controlled process, not for fashionable tool names or avoidable complexity.

## Where the tools help

- **Discovery and structure**: organise information, compare hypotheses, expose missing questions, and
  prepare clearer workshops without inventing customer facts.
- **Design exploration**: prototype and compare several directions earlier, then refine the direction
  that best serves the audience and content.
- **Development**: accelerate repetitive implementation, refactoring, migration, and documentation while
  keeping the accepted architecture and coding standards authoritative.
- **Quality control**: assist code review, test generation, accessibility checks, dependency analysis,
  performance measurement, security scanning, and regression detection.
- **Maintenance**: preserve decisions, produce useful handover material, and make later improvements
  easier to understand and estimate.

## Non-negotiable safeguards

- A human remains responsible for the scope, architecture, design choices, source code, tests, content,
  deployment decision, and delivered result.
- Effective use requires deliberate problem framing, tool selection, context preparation, critical
  evaluation, cross-checking, testing, and the ability to stop or change approach.
- Generated output is treated as an untrusted proposal: it is reviewed, tested, corrected, and rejected
  when it does not meet the requirement.
- Confidential information, credentials, unpublished strategy, personal data, and client content are not
  supplied to an AI service without an explicit need, approved provider, suitable contractual and data
  terms, minimisation, and client information or authorisation where required.
- Third-party licences, asset rights, attribution, and provenance remain part of the review.
- AI is not used to fabricate clients, testimonials, expertise, results, research, or certainty.
- Automation never removes the review points and approvals promised to the client.

## Recommended public copy in French

### Homepage section

**Title**

> L’IA est un outil. Bien l’utiliser est un savoir-faire.

**Body**

> L’intelligence artificielle ne fait pas le travail à ma place. Savoir l’utiliser, c’est comprendre
> le besoin, choisir les bons outils, leur donner le bon contexte, questionner leurs réponses, repérer
> leurs erreurs et vérifier chaque résultat. Associée à l’automatisation, aux tests et aux outils
> modernes de développement, cette maîtrise permet d’explorer davantage de pistes et d’avancer plus
> vite sans sacrifier la qualité. Je reste responsable des choix, du code livré et du résultat.

**Supporting points**

- Plus de pistes explorées avant de choisir une direction.
- Des prototypes et retours plus rapides.
- Davantage de contrôles sur le code, l’accessibilité, les performances et la sécurité.
- Une méthode documentée pour faire évoluer le site plus sereinement.

### FAQ

**Utilisez-vous l’intelligence artificielle pour créer les sites ?**

> Oui, lorsque son utilisation apporte un bénéfice réel. Mais l’IA ne travaille pas seule : il faut
> savoir poser le problème, choisir les outils adaptés, construire le contexte, comparer les réponses,
> détecter les erreurs et tester ce qui est produit. Cette maîtrise permet d’accélérer certaines tâches
> tout en renforçant les contrôles. Les informations confidentielles et les données personnelles ne
> sont pas envoyées à un outil sans cadre adapté, et je reste responsable de ce qui vous est livré.

### Alternative headings

1. **L’IA au service d’un travail plus rapide et plus exigeant** — benefit-led and explicit.
2. **Des outils modernes, une expertise bien humaine** — warmer and trust-led.
3. **Mieux explorer, mieux vérifier, mieux livrer** — useful when AI should not lead the section.

The recommended homepage heading states the differentiating idea directly: access to AI is common,
but using it responsibly and effectively is a professional capability. The alternatives can support
the method and about pages without repeating the same sentence.

## Claims to avoid

- `Built entirely by AI` or any wording that removes human responsibility.
- `Always the latest technology`, which promises novelty rather than suitability and becomes stale.
- Guaranteed speed or quality improvements without measured evidence.
- Named models as the primary differentiator; vendors and capabilities change.
- Claims that AI eliminates errors, cost, review, or specialist expertise.
